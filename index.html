GOOD QUESTIONS

A little clarity.

Still have something on your mind?
Our team is happy to help.

Do you offer emergency plumbing?+

Yes. ASAP Plumbing offers a 24-hour service. For urgent help, call 065 191 8511 and confirm availability with the team.

Which areas do you cover?+

We serve Cape Town and surrounding areas, including the Northern and Southern Suburbs, City Centre, Peninsula, Atlantic Seaboard, Helderberg and Blaauwberg. Contact us to confirm your address.

Is there a call-out fee?+

ASAP Plumbing advertises no call-out fees. The team will discuss the required work and its cost with you. Ask about your specific job when you contact us.

Can you help with geyser insurance claims?+

Yes, we handle geyser installations and deal with insurance claims. Send the team your geyser issue and claim details to discuss the next steps.

How do I request a quote?+

Use the enquiry form below to prepare a WhatsApp message, or call us directly. Include your suburb, the work you need and any helpful photos when you chat to the team.
